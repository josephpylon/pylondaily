PYLON DAILY

ABOUT & PRIVACY

WHAT IT IS

Pylon Daily is a free fantasy football guessing game. You get 7 plays to drive 100 yards. Each play gives you a lineup slot (QB, RB, WR, TE, FLEX or SUPERFLEX) and a number, like 15.6 fantasy points per game. Name the player whose season average is closest to it. The closer you are compared with every other player who fits, the more yards you gain.

Every day at 5 a.m. Eastern there's a new Daily Drive: the same seven plays for everyone, one try. Finish it to unlock free play, other scoring formats, yards per game (passing, rushing, receiving or all three), Rookies Only and Multiplayer.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM

Player stats come from nflverse, an open, community-maintained source of NFL data shared under the CC BY 4.0 license, and refresh automatically every morning during the season. Fantasy points use standard scoring (Full PPR, Half PPR or no PPR) and match the fantasytools.ai leaderboard; passing, rushing and receiving yards were checked against other public stat sites.

If a number looks wrong, use the suggestion box in the game and it gets fixed.

PRIVACY

There are no accounts, no passwords, no ads and no tracking cookies. In short:

  • Saved on your device only: your profile name, stats, Daily results and streak, and your settings. They live in your browser's storage and never leave it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.
  • "You beat X% of players": when you finish the official Daily Drive, the game sends your score for that day (one number) and a random ID made up by your browser, so each result is counted once. No name, no email.
  • "Save anonymous play data": off unless you turn it on, and even then it stays on your device.
  • Suggestions: what you type in the suggestion box (and a name or email only if you add them) is emailed to the person who makes the game through Web3Forms, along with your current game settings.
  • Visitor counts: the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, briefly handles basic request information (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

THE FINE PRINT

Pylon Daily is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NFL, any team or any fantasy football platform. Team names and abbreviations are used only to identify players. The game is for fun: no money, no prizes, no betting.
