PATCH NOTES · NEWEST FIRST
NOVEMBER 17, 2026

FREE PLAY, A SETTINGS MENU, PICK SIXES AND UNLIMITED MODENEW

  • Easier to find free play. After the Daily, the result screen shows a Free Play Unlocked box with one tap into a new drive or Multiplayer.
  • Game settings menu. The clock, what you guess and which players are now in one tidy ⚙ Game settings dropdown, with a line explaining each choice.
  • Sacks and interceptions. Lost yards now say why: a tackle for loss, a sack, or for the very worst answers an interception.
  • Pick six. Name the worst possible player for a play and it's returned for a touchdown: the ball goes back to the start and your drive ends (a touchdown you already scored still counts). In Multiplayer, your opponent gets the touchdown.
  • Unlimited mode ∞. One drive that never runs out of plays: every touchdown counts and the ball goes back to your 0. Play 3 Strikes (sacks, interceptions and missed plays are strikes) to chase your best score, or No Limit to play as long as you like.
NOVEMBER 16, 2026

EASIER TOUCHDOWNS, BONUS YARDS AND TEAM MODE

  • Good answers earn more yards. The closest answer is still worth +35, but near-misses are no longer punished: an answer in the top 5% now gains about 25 yards (was 18), top 10% about 17 (was 8), top 20% about 6 (was 1).
  • Bonus yards. A touchdown no longer ends your drive. Keep going with the plays you have left and every extra yard is added as a bonus, or finish right there. Your touchdown is safe either way.
  • Multiplayer is now a tug-of-war. One ball starts at midfield; one player drives right, the other left. Every touchdown counts and the ball goes back to midfield, so you can keep playing as long as you like.
  • Team mode. In free play, pick one team or one division and every answer has to come from it. Know your roster.
  • Live countdown to the next Daily Drive on the result screen.
  • Better sharing. Shared results show your streak, and “Challenge a friend” sends your score with a dare and the link.
  • Patch notes. This page.
NOVEMBER 15, 2026

LAUNCH DAY

  • pylondaily.com is live. Player stats refresh by themselves every morning.
  • Yards. A second set of options next to fantasy points: Passing, Rushing (quarterbacks included), Receiving, and All yards added together.
  • Home button. Leave any mode and go back to the start screen, except during the official Daily.
  • How to play. A seven-step guide opens on your first visit and any time from the ? button.
  • Rookies Only and Multiplayer (two players, one device).
  • Daily stats and streaks, yesterday's Daily as a replay, and “you beat X% of players today”.
  • Sounds, the three closest answers after every play, and a suggestion box that goes straight to the developer.
  • Hash Marks was the original project name. The game launched as Pylon Daily.
